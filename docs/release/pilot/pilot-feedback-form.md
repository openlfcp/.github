## Questions

Scale questions are 1 (no / not at all) to 5 (yes / fully).

1. **Onboarding — share and join.** Did you share a section and did your
   partner join and insert it, using only the written guide? `yes / no`;
   if no, at which step did you need help? `<…>`
2. **Onboarding help.** How many times did you need help from the
   facilitator to get going? `<count>` and for what? `<…>`
3. **Co-edit.** Did edits by both of you (tasks, subtasks, paragraphs,
   lists) reach the other note as expected? `yes / no`; notes `<…>`
4. **Offline and reconnect.** After editing offline and reconnecting, did
   everything arrive with **nothing missing or duplicated**? `yes / no`;
   if no, describe (no note text) `<…>`
5. **Boundary (comprehension).** In your own words, what is shared in a
   section and what stays private? `<…>` → scorer: correct / partly /
   incorrect
6. **Local-save vs. server-acceptance (comprehension).** What is the
   difference between the ✓✓ "shared" mark and the sync-progress icon? Does
   ✓✓ mean the other person has seen your change? `<…>` → correct / partly /
   incorrect
7. **Detach (comprehension).** What does "Detach this section" do to your
   note and to your partner's copy? `<…>` → correct / partly / incorrect
8. **Access removal (comprehension).** What does "Remove access…" do, and
   can it erase a copy the other person already has? `<…>` → correct /
   partly / incorrect
9. **Conflict exercise.** Did you resolve the practice conflict with "Review
   conflicts…", and was anything lost while you decided? `resolved / not`;
   `nothing lost / something lost` `<…>`
10. **Trust in the status.** When the plugin said something was saved,
    waiting, offline or needed attention, did it match what actually
    happened? (1–5) and any mismatch `<…>`
11. **Blocking problems.** Did you hit anything that stopped you, or any
    sign of lost or wrongly shared content? `yes / no`; if yes, file a
    defect below. (Any unexplained missing/duplicated content or
    private-content exposure is a blocker.)
12. **Overall.** Would the plugin be useful to you once released? (1–5) and
    the one thing you would change `<…>`
