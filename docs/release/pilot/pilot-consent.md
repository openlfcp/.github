# MVP 0.2 pilot — consent (draft)

**Status:** DRAFT (LFCP-02-103), not in use. Plain-language consent for pilot
participants. `<…>` marks a value the owner settles.

## What this is

You are invited to try **Shared Tasks for Obsidian** (version
`0.4.0-beta.17`, a pre-release) with one other person for about **7 days**,
and to tell us how it went. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any
time, for any reason, without giving a reason.

## What you will do

Install a beta of the plugin, share a section of a note with your partner,
edit it together, try it offline, resolve one practice conflict on made-up
content, and answer a few short questions. A short guide walks you through
it.

## What we collect

Only the minimum needed to learn whether the flow works and is understood:

- an **anonymised pair ID** (like `P1`) — not your name;
- the **build ID**, your **operating system** and **Obsidian version**;
- for each step: whether it worked, roughly how long it took, whether you
  needed help, and a short issue code if something went wrong;
- your answers to a few comprehension questions;
- any **sanitised feedback you choose to write**;
- if a problem needs detail, the output of **"Export diagnostics…"**, which
  you preview in full and send **only if you choose to**.

## What we never collect

- the **content of your notes**, note titles, file names or folder paths;
- your **invitation links** or any **keys or secrets**;
- any **automatic upload** of your vault — nothing is sent in the background;
- a contact directory or a profile of you beyond the anonymised pair ID.

"Export diagnostics…" is built to hold **no note text, names, paths, keys or
invitation links**, and it shows you the whole report before anything leaves
your device.

## Beta risks — please read

- This is **pre-release software**. The specifications are still drafts.
  **Do not use it for data you need to protect.**
- **Keep a copy of your vault** before you start, or use a test vault.
- **There is no way back to 0.3.x on a device once 0.4 has started**: the
  first start of 0.4 upgrades and encrypts the plugin's sync data on that
  device, and a 0.3.x plugin can no longer open it. Your notes themselves
  are not changed.
- Shared content is **end-to-end encrypted**, but **removing someone's
  access cannot erase copies they already received**.

at any time. On withdrawal we stop collecting anything new from you and, if
you ask, remove the feedback you have given, except anything already
aggregated anonymously into a summary where it can no longer be singled out.
